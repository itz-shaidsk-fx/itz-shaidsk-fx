Here’s a powerful set of **Extreme Trading Rules & Disciplines** designed for serious, consistent, and focused traders — especially useful when growing small accounts like yours (\$100 to \$1000):

---

## 🔥 **Extreme Trading Rules & Discipline**

### 🔒 1. **Risk Management is Law**

* Never risk more than **5%** per trade (you’re already following this — perfect).
* Set **fixed loss limits per day** (e.g. 2 losses = stop trading).
* Protect capital as if it’s your last bullet.

---

### 🎯 2. **1 Strategy. 1 Setup. No Confusion.**

* Choose **one setup** (e.g., breakout, retest, or support/resistance).
* Master it. Trade nothing else.
* Avoid mixing strategies — clarity builds consistency.

---

### 🧘 3. **Emotion = Enemy**

* Don’t trade when **angry, sad, hyped**, or after big wins/losses.
* If you feel revenge or greed — **walk away immediately**.
* **No "hope" trading** – either setup is there or it isn’t.

---

### ⏳ 4. **No Setup = No Trade**

* **Mark levels** before market opens (like you're doing).
* If your conditions aren’t met, **do nothing**.
* **Discipline > Activity**

---

### 📓 5. **Daily Routine**

* Pre-market analysis
* Mark key levels
* Backtest the setup
* Trade your plan
* Journal every trade (win/loss/emotion/entry/reason)

---

### 🧠 6. **Journal Like a Psychopath**

* Write down:

  * **Why you entered**
  * **Why you exited**
  * **Your feeling during trade**
  * **What went right/wrong**
* Review it **weekly**.

---

### 🛑 7. **Limit Your Trading**

* **Max 2 trades/day** (you’re already doing this — excellent!)
* This avoids overtrading and lets you focus deeply.
* If first trade is a loss, second trade must be **10/10 setup only**.

---

### 🧘‍♂️ 8. **Mindset First**

* Trading is not about being right. It’s about making money.
* Accept losses like fees, not failure.
* Focus on **process**, not outcome.

---

### 🧼 9. **Clean Charts Only**

* No indicators, no clutter.
* Price is king — support/resistance, structure, price action.

---

### 🕵️ 10. **Be Ruthless With Rules**

* Break your rules? **Punish yourself**:

  * No trade next day
  * Reduce lot size
  * Re-review journal 3x
* **Discipline beats motivation**

---

## 🧠 Final Mindset:

**“You don’t trade the market. You trade your rules.”**
